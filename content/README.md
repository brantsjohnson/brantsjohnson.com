# content

Static structured content that is not edited at runtime through the admin portal. Editable projects, media, and posts will live in Supabase instead.

- `site-content.ts` holds the words and facts the public pages show. Pages read from here so copy lives in one place. Dates read "Oct 2024 to Nov 2025".
- `about-brant.md` is the checked career record Ask about me already knows.
- `about-me.md` is the file to fill with anything else you want the chatbot to say. It is read in full.
- `writing/posts.ts` is the essay and newsletter shelf. Leave it empty until a real piece exists.
