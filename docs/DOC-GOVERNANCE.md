# Document governance

## Source-of-truth hierarchy
Founder-approved commitments and explicit policy/security requirements take precedence. Named subject documents in INDEX govern their domain. Current BUILD-STATE reports factual implementation but cannot overrule founder intent or approved design. Proposed notes and chats are not accepted product law.

## Context discipline
Tier 0: AGENTS, INDEX, top of BUILD-STATE. Tier 1: only relevant specifications listed in INDEX. Tier 2: research and archived decisions when needed. Before coding, inspect missing gaps and the existing design/source.

## Reconciliation
On conflict, cite both exact locations in DECISIONS, request a ruling when necessary, and update all affected documents, references, tests, schema map and contracts in the same PR. Do not silently pick a winner or trim accepted requirements.

## History
Keep dated amendments and superseding ADRs. Mark rejected, planned, active, approved, shipped or Not applicable separately. A file being present or AI-generated does not indicate founder sign-off.
